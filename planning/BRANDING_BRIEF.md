# Branding Brief: App Naming Project

## Overview
We need a compelling, memorable name for our AI-powered positioning and voice capture platform designed for content agencies and their clients.

---

## What the App Does

### Core Functionality
Our app is an **AI-powered interview system** that helps agencies capture their clients' unique positioning, voice, and differentiation through conversational interviews. The system then synthesizes comprehensive "Category of One" profiles that agencies use to create authentic, on-brand content at scale.

### The Problem It Solves
Content agencies struggle to:
- Capture clients' authentic voice and positioning beyond surface-level information
- Scale content production while maintaining voice fidelity across multiple platforms
- Onboard new clients efficiently with deep strategic understanding
- Create content that truly reflects each client's unique "Category of One" positioning

### The Solution
1. **Dynamic AI Interview**: Conducts intelligent, adaptive conversations with clients to uncover:
   - Their unique positioning (who they serve, what transformation they deliver, how they do it)
   - Their contrarian beliefs and differentiation
   - Their voice DNA (tone, language patterns, distinctive phrases)
   - Their methodology and frameworks
   - Proof points and transformation stories

2. **Intelligent Synthesis**: Transforms interview transcripts into structured positioning profiles including:
   - Positioning statements
   - Unique differentiation
   - Contrarian approach
   - Transformation narratives
   - Voice and language guidelines
   - Competitive landscape analysis

3. **Agency Management**: Provides admin tools for:
   - Client onboarding and management
   - Brand knowledge integration
   - AI prompt configuration (no-code customization)
   - Profile review and export

### Use Case
Agencies use this to:

## Brand Personality & Tone

### Desired Characteristics
- **Professional yet approachable**: Enterprise-grade tool that feels intuitive
- **Strategic**: Positioned as a strategic positioning tool, not just a chatbot
- **Precise**: Focused on accuracy and depth, not hype
- **Agency-focused**: Built for professionals who understand positioning and voice
- **Innovative**: Leverages cutting-edge AI in a thoughtful, purposeful way

### What We're NOT
- Not a generic chatbot or AI assistant
- Not a content generation tool (it creates profiles/guides, not content)
- Not a consumer-facing app
- Not focused on speed over depth

---

## Naming Considerations

### Must-Haves
- Memorable and brandable
- Conveys depth/intelligence (not surface-level)
- Suggests positioning/strategy/voice capture
- Professional enough for B2B agency market
- Available domain potential

### Nice-to-Haves
- Alludes to "Category of One" concept
- Suggests interview/conversation/discovery
- Implies synthesis or transformation
- Short (1-3 words preferred)

### Avoid
- Generic AI/chatbot names
- Overly technical jargon
- Names that suggest content generation (we create profiles, not content)
- Names that sound consumer-focused
- Overly long or complex names
